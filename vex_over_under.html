VEX Robotics: Over Under (UTK Ynot Robotics)

Organization: Ynot Robotics Club, University of Tennessee, Knoxville
Role: Design Lead & Vice President
Competition: VEX Robotics Competition — Over Under
Result: Qualified for VEX Worlds (unable to attend due to travel cost)

Overview

During my freshman year at UTK, I led the mechanical design effort for our VEX Over Under robots and served as Vice President of the club. The project involved rapid CAD iteration, prototyping, mechanism refinement, and reliability improvements driven by on-field performance.

Robot Photo

Two competition robots developed for the VEX Over Under season.

My Responsibilities

  • Owned mechanical design direction and coordinated design decisions across the team
  • Led design meetings and organized iteration priorities based on match performance
  • Drove concept selection and mechanism refinement with a focus on reliability and repeatability
  • Supported fabrication and assembly planning to minimize downtime between events
  • Helped secure resources/sponsorships and managed planning as Vice President

Engineering Focus Areas

  • Design iteration: Rapid changes based on real match feedback
  • Packaging: Component placement for robustness, serviceability, and weight balance
  • Mechanism reliability: Reinforcement, alignment, and fastener retention
  • Systems thinking: Integrating drivetrain + mechanisms while protecting critical components

Outcome

  • Robots performed strongly across the season and qualified for VEX Worlds
  • Built competition-ready designs with an emphasis on repeatable operation and fast serviceability
  • Developed team workflow for design reviews, iteration, and build support

Key Skills Demonstrated

  • Mechanical design leadership and team communication
  • Prototype-to-competition iteration cycle
  • Design for manufacturing and assembly (DFM/DFA)
  • System integration, reliability, and troubleshooting
